Welcome, Lagos explorer!

Choose two public places and plan a route. Press Start, then steer with arrow keys or WASD, or use the large touch controls. Hold the accelerator to move. Switch to Guided to watch your vehicle follow the route automatically.

Map View is overhead; Street View follows your vehicle from an angled camera. This is a map-based prototype, so buildings are approximate 3D blocks. Real photos opens an external site when images are available.

Safety: This is a game, not walking or driving guidance. Do not enter private home addresses.
